add an account

hand over the admin role

There is one admin. The account you choose becomes it, and you become a curator.

roles

reader reads and downloads, keeps its own progress and reader settings.

curator also organises the library: moves, trash, details, renames, uploads, conversions (from M6 on).

admin also adds and removes accounts, sets roles and edits library settings.

Everyone has a private folder, /library/~name: the libraries in it are seen by that account alone. Removing an account leaves its folder on disk, unseen, until an account of that name exists again.

Sign-in through a proxy: on, header Remote-User, trusted from 10.0.0.2 only (set with --trust-header and --trust-proxy). A name the proxy sends that has no account gets one, as a reader.
