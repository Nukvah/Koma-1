how it works

A delete moves the files into /library/.koma-trash, on the same drive, so it is instant and nothing is copied. The books leave the library at once; their ids, reading positions and edits are kept with them.

Restore puts them back where they were, making any folder that has gone since, and adds “(2)” to a name that is taken now. They come back with their progress.

After the keep time they are erased by the worker. “Delete for good” and “empty the trash” ask once more, and cannot be undone.

Curators and the admin see every shared library's trash; your own folder's trash is yours alone.
